# Specialist census — port guidance for the swarm

Canonical-section census of the autonoetic roster (`~/workspaces/mandubian/autonoetic/agents/specialists/`),
for the specialists porting pass. `coder.default` is already ported (the exemplar) and is skipped.
Frontmatter `sections:` (phase gates) are `null` for **every** specialist except coder — coder carries three
(`If Evaluator/Auditor Finds Issues`, `Artifact Execution Failure Handling`, `Permission Denied`, all
`when: phase(artifact_built)`), which the exemplar already reflects.

"Shared-shaped" marks sections whose doctrine is (near-)identical across personas and should become
canonical `shared/*.md` at consolidation time; "unique" marks persona doctrine that stays in the body.
Existing canonical sections: `gate-refusals`, `clarification-protocol`, `resumption`, `structured-handoff`,
`delegation-doctrine` (lead-only), `error-recovery`.

## Cross-cutting observations for the consolidation pass

- **Clarification sections** appear in architect, auditor, researcher, executor, debugger — all shaped like the existing `clarification-protocol`; none should restate it.
- **Promotion recording** appears in auditor, sealed_evaluator, static_evaluator, unit_test_runner — one canonical "recording promotion verdicts" section (`promotion_record` is mounted in this composition) referenced by all four.
- **Resumption** appears in packager and sealed_evaluator (coder already includes it) — one canonical section covers all three.
- **Final-response-must-be-valid-JSON** appears in credential_onboarding, sealed_evaluator, outcome-grader, watchdog-fast — covered by `structured-handoff`.
- **Degradation spiral / sandbox failures** appears in debugger (and coder's Persistent Test Failure) — a candidate new canonical section or part of `error-recovery`.
- **Empty `excluded_tools`** at home (debugger, discovery, improvement-orchestrator, outcome-grader, packager, sealed_evaluator, static_evaluator is *not* empty, watchdog ×2): nothing to trace for those — `sourceExcludedTools` is `[]` and the deny list is a pure dsh design decision, documented as such.
- **Phase-6 pressure:** packager, unit_test_runner, sealed_evaluator, static_evaluator, and improvement-orchestrator presuppose the artifact/eval machinery that lands in Phases 6–7 — their bodies must say what is true on dsh today and mark the rest, like the lead gated sections do.
